QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Everything you should know before you begin.

Is the interactive preview my finished design?

No. It is a free illustrative concept. Final work is prepared after you approve a written quote and project scope.

What exactly will I receive?

Your selected package lists the number of promotional graphics, captions, and planning materials. Final file formats and delivery method are confirmed in writing before work begins; editable source files are not automatically included.

How long does a project take?

The Business Refresh example proposes approximately 72 hours after all required materials and project confirmation, subject to availability. Other timelines are quoted individually and are not guaranteed until confirmed.

What counts as a revision?

A revision is a round of requested changes to the agreed concept. A completely new concept, new deliverables, or changes outside the approved scope may require a separate quote. We confirm these terms before starting.

Are there extra fees or advertising costs?

Published package prices are introductory proposals, not an automatic checkout price. Paid ads, printing, account management, taxes, or extra work are not included unless specified. Any additional charges must be disclosed before approval.

Can I cancel or request a refund?

Cancellation and refund eligibility depend on the agreed project stage and written terms. We will provide those terms for review before accepting payment. No payment is collected through this preview website.

Can I use the finished designs commercially?

Commercial usage rights, any third-party asset licenses, and whether source files transfer are specified in the written agreement. Do not assume that demo designs or stock assets grant exclusive ownership.

How do I get started?

Explore the studio, fill in the quote request, review the draft in your email application, and send it to BelanQora. We will respond with next steps; no order is placed automatically.
